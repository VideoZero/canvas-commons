Add `@canvas-commons/three`: Three.js 3D scenes as nodes. `Three` is a viewport
with an orbit camera, fog and shadows; `Mesh3D`, `Group3D`, `Light3D` and
`Object3D` are 3D nodes driven by signals in pixel units. 2D children of a
`Mesh3D` are drawn onto its surface.
